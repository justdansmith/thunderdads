What we're building this year

A few of us got together at Taps & Take Out to pick what Thunderdads works on this year. Here's where we landed, and what's already done.

What we're working on

  • Shade for the portables is our big project. Retractable awnings that dads can install. We start with one and add more as the money comes in.
  • Turf for the yard, with school partners. Partners are leading it, and we bring the hands on install day.
  • Kids Night Out keeps getting better. December 4 is next: a quick check-in at the door, a job for every dad, and a playbook so anyone can lead the next one.
  • Fix-it workdays. One list of jobs, a Saturday morning, and as many dads as we can get.

How we roll

  • Everyone's welcome. Dads, father figures, and every caregiver.
  • A lead for every event. The lead makes sure everything gets done, with a playbook anyone can pick up.
  • Regular meetups. A set day, so it's easy to plan around.
  • Keep it fun. If it isn't fun, nobody comes back.
