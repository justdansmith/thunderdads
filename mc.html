Before the doors open

Welcome

Do

Wait until most kids are here. Start big.

Say

Hello, Thornhill! Welcome to Kids Night Out!

Do

Cup your ear and look disappointed.

Say

I've heard louder cheering from a library full of sleeping sloths. Let's try that again!

Say

My name is [your name]. I'm one of the Thunder Dads, and I'm [your kid]'s dad.

Say

Tonight we've got games, pizza, and a movie. Everybody wave goodbye to the grown-ups. Bye, grown-ups!

The signal

Say

When I say Thunder, you say BOOM, and the room goes silent, like a storm that just stopped. Thunder!

Do

Freeze, finger to lips, wait for silence.

Say

Now whisper it. Now the loudest BOOM in Thornhill history. And careful, I'm tricky. Thunder-pants!

Do

Half the room yells BOOM. Laugh, catch them once more, then a real Thunder.

Every time: wait in silence until the room is quiet, then thank the first row that froze.

Backups: "Macaroni and cheese!" and "Everybody freeze!" Or "Hocus pocus!" and "Everybody focus!"

Rules

Do

Hold up a finger for each rule. Let them guess it first.

Say

One: we stay in the room. Need the bathroom? [bathroom rule].

Say

Two: walking feet. We walk around sleeping bags, never over them.

Say

Three: kind hands, kind words. See someone alone? Invite them in.

Say

Four: when you hear Thunder, you BOOM and freeze.

Quick quiz, thumbs up or down

  • Can I go get a snack from the car?No!
  • Can I put pizza in my pocket for later?NO!
  • Can I bounce on someone's sleeping bag?NO!
  • If someone's alone, do we invite them?Yes!

Someone runs? Call "Rewind!" and have them walk it back in slow motion.

Games

Play in the open half of the room. Nobody stays out for long.

Would You Rather, wall to wall

Say

Pick one by walking (walking!) to that wall.

  1. Pet dragon or pet unicorn
  2. Pizza for breakfast or ice cream for dinner
  3. Fly or be invisible
  4. Spaghetti hair or sweat maple syrup
  5. No homework or no bedtime

End with: "Would you rather sit down quietly right now, or sit down quietly right now?"

Thunder Says

Best for TK to 2nd. Simon Says, but Thunder is the boss: touch your nose, flap like a chicken, T. rex roar. Caught kids do a silly dance and jump back in.

Freeze Dance

Music plays, they dance. Music stops, they freeze. Rounds: robot, ghost, spaghetti noodle, slow motion.

Stand Up If

The lead-in to pizza. Stand up if you have a dog. If you lost a tooth this year. If you're hungry for pizza.

Rock Paper Scissors tournament Optional

Loud, and nobody is out. Try it if the energy dips. Lose a round and you become the winner's cheerleader, chanting their name. Winners face winners until two kids face off with half the room cheering for each.

Before the movie

Sleeping Lions: everyone lies still while helpers try to make them giggle, no touching. Whisper countdown: ten to one, quieter each number, then lights down.

Jokes

Say

If a joke is bad, you groan as loud as you can. Let's practice. GROAN!

  • What do you call cheese that isn't yours?Nacho cheese.
  • Want to hear a pizza joke?Never mind, it's too cheesy.
  • Why didn't the skeleton go to the party?He had no body to go with.
  • What room does a ghost not need?A living room.
  • What do you call a witch at the beach?A sand-witch.
  • What do you call a sleeping dinosaur?A dino-snore.
  • Knock knock. Interrupting cow. Interrupting c...MOO!
  • Knock knock. Boo. Boo who?Don't cry, it's just Kids Night Out!

Joke Mic: kids whisper their joke to you first, then tell the room.

End of night

Two rules: everyone stays on or beside their sleeping bag, and nothing flies.

  • Monster Mash-Up. Fold paper in three. One kid draws a head, folds, passes. Next draws a body, then legs. Unfold and name it.
  • Draw Along. "Draw a big circle. Two pointy ears. Three eyes. A giant smile with one tooth. Name your monster!"
  • Telephone. Whisper "The purple dinosaur ate seventeen pickles" around the circle.
  • Story Circle. "One night at Kids Night Out, a giant slice of pizza walked in and said..." One sentence each.
  • Heads Up, Seven Up. Heads down, thumbs up. Seven kids tiptoe and press one thumb each. Pressed kids guess who.

Pickup

Pickup at [pickup time]

Say

Roll up your sleeping bag and sit on it. When your grown-up arrives, stay put until a helper calls your name. Nobody walks out alone.

Say

Thunderous applause for our volunteers! Thunder! BOOM! Thanks for coming to Kids Night Out!

If it gets loud

  • Too loud. Signal once, then wait in silence. Drop to a whisper.
  • Praise what you want. "This row froze in one second" beats "Stop talking."
  • One kid won't listen. Quiet word at eye level. Offer two good choices.
  • Big kids bored. Make them Thunder Captains: napkins, popcorn, leading lines.
  • Worried little one. Give them a job, like crayon passer or joke assistant.
