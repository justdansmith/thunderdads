Thunderdads and the PFC:
a working agreement

Thunderdads stays a PFC committee. The membership votes it a standing allocation, and inside that allocation the dads choose their projects and run their own rules.

In brief

  • Mission. Thunderdads brings Thornhill dads and guardians together to build, fix, and fund the things that make our school and community better for our kids.
  • Vision. Dads and guardians who show up, kids who feel a whole community behind them, and a school that gets better every year because of it.
  • Standing. A committee of the PFC. The PFC holds the bank account, the Treasurer keeps the books, and Kids Night Out runs under the PFC's insurance.
  • Money. The membership votes one allocation: what Kids Night Out nets this school year sits in the Thunderdads line, and the dads direct it under a written charter.
  • The split. Of what Thunderdads nets: 75 to 80% to one big project, turf or shade; 10 to 15% to small fixes and workdays around the school; and a share for requests the PFC brings, proposed at up to 10%, for the dads to agree.
  • Leads. ProposalScott Johnson and Dan Smith lead the club for the rest of this school year. The dads elect co-chairs in spring 2027.
  • Principal. Anything that touches campus has Principal Scott's sign-off before money is spent.

Where we agree, and the gap

Everyone has already said the same four things: the money sits in the PFC account, Thunderdads is a PFC committee, a written plan comes first, and the principal signs off on campus work.

Limits on social spending

  • Off campus only.
  • Open to every Thornhill dad and guardian, and announced ahead of time.
  • Both leads approve the amount before the night.
  • Receipts go to the Treasurer, and the total appears in every report to the PFC.
  • The yearly total stays under the cap.

How a project gets funded

1. Every request answers six questions

  1. What is it, and who does it help?
  2. How much, and what does it buy?
  3. When is the money needed?
  4. Will dads do any of the work?
  5. Who is asking, and who owns it?
  6. Does the principal support it, and does the district need to approve it?

2. A good project for Thunderdads

  • Helps Thornhill kids or teachers directly.
  • Uses dads' time or skills, beyond money.
  • Can be finished or funded this school year.

4. Paying and reporting

  • Reimbursement is by the PFC expense form, to the PFC Treasurer.
  • The club Treasurer reports money in, money out, and what is planned at every club meeting.

  • One decision log: date, request, amount, decision, owner, receipt filed.

Charter

For the club meeting in early December, then PFC board approval.

1. Name and standing

Thunderdads is a standing committee of the Thornhill Parent Faculty Club, appointed by the PFC President with the approval of the board. It operates under the PFC bylaws. Art. II, Sec. 2a and 7d

2. Mission and vision

Mission. Thunderdads brings Thornhill dads and guardians together to build, fix, and fund the things that make our school and community better for our kids.

Vision. Dads and guardians who show up, kids who feel a whole community behind them, and a school that gets better every year because of it.

Thunderdads works in partnership with the PFC and the principal.

3. Membership

Open to every Thornhill dad, father figure, and caregiver. No dues.

4. Leadership

  • ProposalFor 2026-27, Scott Johnson and Dan Smith lead the club and are its contacts for the PFC and the principal. The first co-chair election is in spring 2027.
  • From 2027-28, two co-chairs serve two school years each, starting a year apart. They approve spending together and are the club's contact for the PFC and the principal. Shared positions: Art. II, Sec. 4
  • A Treasurer keeps the club's record of money in and out. The PFC Treasurer keeps the official books and the bank account.
  • Every event has a lead and a written playbook.
  • Co-chairs are elected at a club meeting announced three weeks ahead, with a way to vote by proxy.

5. Meetings and votes

The club meets on a set day, monthly or every other month. A decision passes by a majority of the dads voting, at a meeting or in a 48-hour poll.

6. Money

  • All money is held by the PFC. Thunderdads spends only inside the allocation the PFC membership has voted, and makes no commitment on the PFC's behalf beyond it. Art. II, Sec. 7a and 7c
  • Of the net, 75 to 80% goes to one big project such as turf or shade, 10 to 15% to small projects around the school, and a share, proposed at up to 10%, is set aside for requests the PFC brings.
  • Spending follows the limits in "Who decides" and "Limits on social spending" above.
  • Each spring the club reviews the split for the next school year.

7. Working with the PFC and the principal

  • Thunderdads chooses and leads its own projects.
  • Nothing is built, installed, or changed on campus without the principal's sign-off, and district approval where it applies.
  • The club sends its project list and budget to the PFC presidents each February for the May budget vote.
  • Fundraising dates are set with the PFC so they help the auction and other PFC events.

8. Events

  • School events such as Kids Night Out run as PFC events and follow PFC and district rules for volunteers and facilities.
  • Social nights are off-campus club gatherings. They are not PFC events.

9. Records and continuity

  • Co-chairs keep a shared folder for the next co-chairs: playbooks, vendors, budgets, and the decision log. Art. IV
  • If the club has no co-chairs for a full school year, unspent money in its line returns to the PFC's general use.

10. Changes

The club changes this charter by a vote at a meeting announced three weeks ahead. Changes go to the PFC board.
