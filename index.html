Why a dads group

Thornhill has plenty of work that needs more hands. Thunder Dads gives every dad a simple way in: help at a Kids Night Out, take a fix-it job, or help raise money for something bigger.

Free to join, no minimum. Give an hour or a whole Saturday.

What we do

Kids Night Out

A monthly evening for Thornhill kids, with pizza and volunteer parents in charge, while parents get a night off. Parents who stay and help watch the kids come free.

Fix-it projects

Teachers and staff tell us what's broken, like wobbly desks, tired chairs, or the picket fence, and a dad takes the job on a workday.

Bigger projects

This year's big project is a turf area for the yard, and shade is on the list too. The dads raise the money and work with the school and the district to make them happen.
