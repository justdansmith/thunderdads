Why a dads group

Thornhill has plenty of work that needs more hands. Thunderdads gives every dad a simple way in: help at a Kids Night Out, take a fix-it job, or help raise money for something bigger.

Free to join, no minimum. Give an hour or a whole Saturday.

Our mission
Thunderdads brings Thornhill dads and guardians together to build, fix, and fund the things that make our school and community better for our kids.
Our vision
Dads and guardians who show up, kids who feel a whole community behind them, and a school that gets better every year because of it.

What we believe

Kids do better when dads show up at school. In a federal survey of K-12 families in two-parent households, half of students got mostly A's and enjoyed school when their fathers were highly involved at school, compared with about a third when fathers had low involvement. National Center for Education Statistics, 1998

Dads do better with a crew. In a national survey, 15 percent of men said they have no close friends, five times the share in 1990. Thunderdads is an inclusive group that gives dads and guardians a reason to get together and build something for our school community. Survey Center on American Life

What we do

Kids Night Out

A Friday evening for Thornhill kids, with pizza, a movie, and the dads in charge, while parents get a night off. Kids come free when a parent stays for the night.

Fix-it projects

Teachers and staff tell us what's broken, like wobbly desks, tired chairs, or the picket fence, and a dad takes the job on a workday.

Bigger projects

This year's big project is shade for the portables, and the dads will help build the new turf area with school partners. We raise the money and work with the school and the district to make them happen.

Kids Night Out

A night off for parents, and three hours of pizza, popcorn, and a movie for the kids. Every Thornhill kid is welcome. Sponsored tickets are available: reach out to Principal Scott, no questions asked.

Join Thunderdads

Scott Johnson leads Thunderdads. Questions go to thunderdads@thornhillschool.org.

  1. Tell us you're in

    Be as casual as you like: come when you can, just for the social nights, for a half-day project, or about once a month. One short form.

    Fill out the join form

  2. Join the WhatsApp community

    Groups for Kids Night Out, Walkathon, Auction Stuff, Thunder Games, and Announcements. Once your form comes in, one of the dads texts you the invite.

  3. Come to a meetup

    A full club meeting in December, date to be confirmed. On the list: planning a work day at school and social gatherings. Dads, father figures, and every caregiver are welcome.
