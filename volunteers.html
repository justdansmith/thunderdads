Thunderdads, Thornhill Elementary

Kids Night Out volunteer jobs

Friday, December 4, doors 6:00 PM, pickup 9:00 PM, in the multipurpose room. Nine volunteers cover the door, pizza, popcorn, the MC, the tech, and close-out. Find your job and read it once before the night.

Sign up for a job

The jobs

Door2 people

Every kid in, every kid out, on the door sheet. You hold the lobby door all night.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. Set up the table at the lobby door. It is the only way in or out.
  2. Open the door sheet on your phone. Keep the printed copy and a pen on the table.
  3. Drop-off: find the kid on the sheet, tick In, and confirm the pickup adults with the parent. Tell the pizza dads about any allergies.
  4. Walk-ups buy on their phone from the sign on the table. Cash is the fallback: add the kid as a new row marked Cash, with a pickup adult and a phone number.
  5. During the movie, one of you stays at the door and the other sits with the art desks in the lobby.
  6. Pickup at 9:00 PM: match the adult to the pickup list, then tick Out.

Done when: The door sheet shows nobody still here.

Pizza2 people

Hot pizza on the table by 6:35, and every kid fed.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. Partner: set the water cooler near the pizza table with a towel under the spout, and lay out plates, napkins, and cups.
  2. Runner: leave at 6:10 PM to pick up the order at Cybelle's. Pizza on the table by 6:35 PM.
  3. Keep the allergy list from the door dads at the pizza table. Gluten-free pizzas sit apart.
  4. At 6:40 PM serve one row at a time, one slice each first. Seconds once everyone has one.
  5. Boxes flattened and trash bagged once the movie is running.

Done when: Leftovers boxed, table wiped, trash bagged.

Popcorn2 people

Popcorn in every kid's hands as they sit down.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. One machine, on its own outlet. Two on one outlet trip the breaker.
  2. Start popping at 5:30 PM and fill the bags, so there is a stockpile when doors open.
  3. From 6:00 PM: a bag of popcorn and a cup of water for each kid as they settle. Keep popping until the pizza comes out.
  4. During the movie, walk kids to the bathroom when they need it.

Done when: Machine off, cooled, wiped, back in the closet.

MC1 person

Run the room: welcome, rules, pizza rows, and the closing games.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. Teach the Thunder and BOOM signal to the other volunteers before kids arrive.
  2. Check that every door except the lobby door is shut.
  3. 6:35 PM: welcome, the signal, and the rules. Then call rows for pizza.
  4. Tell the kids about the art desks in the lobby for a break from the movie.
  5. After the movie: kids roll their sleeping bags and help clean up, then freeze dance and limbo. Nothing flies, balloons included.

Done when: The last kid is picked up.

Tech1 person

Picture and sound work, and the movie starts on time.

Arrive: 5:15 PM

  1. Before the night: movie and cartoon shorts downloaded to the laptop and played through once.
  2. Screen down, projector, sound, and wireless mic tested before the other dads arrive.
  3. Shorts playing by 5:55 PM. Pause them for the MC's welcome at 6:35 PM.
  4. Start the movie at 6:50 PM on the MC's cue, and stay within reach of the laptop.
  5. Music for freeze dance and limbo after the movie.

Done when: Laptop, projector, mic, and cables packed and back in the closet.

Close-out1 person

Leave the room cleaner than we found it.

Arrive: 8:15 PM, during the movie

  1. Quietly bag trash and stack empty boxes while the movie plays.
  2. After pickup: brooms and supplies from the closet, sweep the room, wipe the tables, put the art desks back.
  3. Everything back in the closet: popcorn machine, extension cords, brooms, games.
  4. Lost and found into one bag for the office.
  5. Lock up: Who locks the room: Dan confirms.

Done when: Room swept, closet packed, lights off.

  • Know the exits and where the first-aid kit is.
