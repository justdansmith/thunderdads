Thunder Dads, Thornhill Elementary

Kids Night Out volunteer jobs

Friday, December 4, doors 6:00 PM, pickup 9:00 PM, in the multipurpose room. Eight volunteers cover check-in, pizza, popcorn, the MC, and close-out. Find your job and read it once before the night.

Sign up for a job

The jobs

Check-in2 people

Every kid in, every kid out, on the list.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. Set up the check-in table by the door with the printed ticket list as backup.
  2. Open the Givebutter app on your phone. Test that you can see names, not just the ticket total, before doors.
  3. Doors at 6:00 PM: scan each ticket's QR code, or search the family name. Read the allergy and pickup notes on the ticket.
  4. Tell the pizza crew about any allergies. Walk-ups can buy on the Givebutter page on their phone.
  5. At pickup, 9:00 PM: release each kid only to an adult on that ticket's pickup list, and mark them out on the paper list.

Done when: Every name on the list is marked out.

Pizza2 people

Food on the table by the time the games end.

Arrive: 5:30 PM, runner at the pizza place

  1. Runner: pick up the order at Pizza place: Dan confirms and have it on the table by 6:30 PM.
  2. Partner: set the water cooler near the pizza table with a towel under the spout, and lay out plates and napkins.
  3. Keep the allergy notes from check-in at the pizza table.
  4. Serve one row at a time, one slice each first. Seconds once everyone has one.
  5. Boxes and trash bagged before the movie starts.

Done when: Leftovers boxed, table wiped, trash bagged.

Popcorn2 people

Popcorn ready as the movie starts.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. Before doors: popcorn machine plugged in and tested, bags or cups and kernels out.
  2. Help the MC with games and sleeping bag rows until about 7:00 PM.
  3. Around 7:00 PM start popping. The MC calls popcorn row by row, and you bring it to the sleeping bags.
  4. During the movie, walk kids to the bathroom when they need it.

Done when: Machine off, cooled, wiped, back in the closet.

MC1 person

Run the room: welcome, rules, games, movie.

Arrive: 5:30 PM

  1. Computer, slides, projector, and sound tested before doors.
  2. Run the night from the MC card: welcome, signal, rules, games, pizza, popcorn, movie.
  3. Teach the Thunder and BOOM signal to the other volunteers before kids arrive.
  4. Nothing flies tonight, balloons included.

Done when: Computer and projector packed, closet door shut.

Close-out1 person

Leave the room cleaner than we found it.

Arrive: 8:15 PM, during the movie

  1. Quietly bag trash and stack empty boxes while the movie plays.
  2. After pickup: brooms and supplies from the closet, sweep the room, wipe the tables.
  3. Everything back in the closet: popcorn machine, extension cords, brooms, games.
  4. Lost and found into one bag for the office.
  5. Lock up: Who locks the room: Dan confirms.

Done when: Room swept, closet packed, lights off.
